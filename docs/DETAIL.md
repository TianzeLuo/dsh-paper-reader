Typical use: import one PDF, read the report at brief, standard or deep depth, then ask follow-up questions whose answers cite page and section; figures are cropped and placed where they are first mentioned.

Limits: PDFs only; figure detection may miss a panel or frame too much, which a manual crop box fixes; formulas lose super- and subscripts in text extraction, so they are re-typed as LaTeX and checked against the page.
